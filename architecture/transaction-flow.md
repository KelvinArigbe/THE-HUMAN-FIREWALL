
# Meridian Bank — Transaction Workflow

> Educational simulation. Meridian Bank is fictional.

## 1. Transaction lifecycle

1. Customer submits a payment instruction.
2. Bank receives the instruction.
3. Document and signatory mandate checks are performed.
4. Bank staff contact the registered signatory.
5. Signatory confirms the transaction amount and beneficiary.
6. Required management approvals are determined by transaction value.
7. Bank staff contact the required approvers by telephone.
8. Verbal approvals are documented in the banking system.
9. Processing staff verify that all required approvals are complete.
10. Transaction is released for processing.

## 2. Approval hierarchy

| Transaction category | Required approvals |
|---|---|
| Low value | Account Officer |
| Medium value | Account Officer + Group Head |
| High value | Account Officer + Group Head + Divisional Head |

Actual monetary thresholds are intentionally excluded.

## 3. Verification requirements

- Confirm the customer's identity through an approved independent process.
- Verify the payment amount.
- Verify beneficiary details.
- Confirm the required management approvals.
- Ensure disputed instructions remain on hold.
- Maintain an attributable approval record.

## 4. Important security consideration

Telephone confirmation alone should not be treated as sufficient proof of identity or authorisation.

The simulation will evaluate independent identity verification, transaction-specific confirmation, approval integrity and auditability.

## 5. Scope limitation

This workflow is a fictional educational reconstruction based on general banking experience. It does not represent the verified internal procedures of any particular financial institution.
